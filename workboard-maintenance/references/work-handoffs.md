# Work rounds and handoffs

Use when extracting current actions, updating their context or preparing work
the user can actually continue. Preserve the existing board and its schemas;
this reference does not create a new Inbox, collector or permission grant.

## Select and reconcile

- To-do answers whose turn it is and what bounded action can advance now.
  Project Next steps is another entrance to that same record/state. Progress
  explains consequential evolution, not a checklist of every project step.
- Read the direct request and later replies, then directly relevant authorized
  documents, review segments and confirmed project decisions. Use the configured
  source map, not an indiscriminate mailbox sweep.
- Distinguish execution, design choice, factual gap and no new action.
  A mention can be FYI. A source gap is not an assignment to the user.
  A hypothesis is not a product commitment.
- Merge supplemental messages, replies and drafts by the work problem, not by
  message count. Preserve stable IDs when an ask acquires clearer scope.
- Waiting goes to dependencies. An answered initial question may lead to a real
  next action, not another “reply for the first time” task. Retire only with
  supported completion/supersession or an explicit scope change; age or an
  elapsed deadline alone cannot silently remove an unresolved request.
- Keep personal plans, completion, notes, hides and archive decisions. Do not
  automatically reopen archived projects or invent a task for a shipped project.
  Project archive alone does not complete or suppress a freshly reconciled,
  explicit current action: keep that action eligible without unarchiving its
  project. Personal hiding, completion, planning and suggestion acceptance still
  apply; do not revive stale, waiting, superseded or unreviewed records.

## Prepare something useful

Small execution tasks remain execution tasks. Larger work gets a concrete round:
settle a bounded discussion, compare an evidenced choice, or prepare an agreed
review. Do not split reading, finding references and making notes into user tasks,
or rewrite everything as “review AI draft”. Complex collaboration need not become
a forced A/B choice or wait for a complete spec.

Choose the artifact to fit the question: clarification/reply draft, direction
comparison, flow/state table, copy, test plan or appropriate editable artifact.
For choices, provide evidence, recommendation, alternatives and what would change
the recommendation. A factual clarification should address the actual gap and
an evidenced owner, not a guessed person.

Only claim preparation when it exists and is useful. Verify local file/hash or
actual native-tool identity; no placeholder links, imaginary frames or a text
brief presented as a working prototype. Reconcile changed source content before
reusing an old preparation, including edits under the same source ID.

The action remains visible while AI prepares. Reading views contain concise,
confirmed requests/current actions or actual completed work. Direct user
commitments and verified outcomes from Codex can support those facts; assistant
brainstorming, chapter plans and unaccepted choices cannot. Keep the agreed
deliverable instead of replacing it with “review AI draft” or “decide the outline.”

**Keep the approved structure; curate the contents.** Task details use current
action → what happened → prepared content → user judgment. Show concise real
context, actual-artifact summaries/links and only supported current choices.
Useful AI preparation is allowed as preparation, not an adopted decision or a
new task. Empty sections are optional; routine execution need not become a choice.
Do not regress a concrete next action to an old broad deliverable name.
This supersedes the overbroad instruction to remove preparation/judgment sections
entirely when the user only rejected their uncurated contents.

Do not automatically replay raw preparation bodies, assistant questions,
done/checked/unknown audit logs or local discussion excerpts. Use an explicitly
reviewed projection bound to current task/evidence and verified artifacts; stale
content needs reconciliation. Preserve private records and personal state.
Completion follows personal state or verified outcomes, not AI readiness.
The scan line still contains real deadlines, explicit counterparts or waiting.

## Verify the whole requested scope

For an all-project migration, audit source, custom, legacy and archived projects;
keep a private per-project disposition/preparation/gap record. Check actual
expanded content and shared-state behavior, not just a schema or one example.
Evidence-backed full current-source coverage is distinct from a complete local
inventory audit. Record partial reads and prior blockers honestly.

Use the board's candidate/content/state-preserving gates. A deterministic staging
validator is not an autonomous source reader or cross-tool agent. New automation,
external sending, publishing, formal design edits and access changes are separate
actions requiring their own authorization.
